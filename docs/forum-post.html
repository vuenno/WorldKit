WorldKit

Keep your chat logs. Turn them into in-game-looking chat screenshots. Build your RP shots in one place.

What is it?

WorldKit is a free Windows app for GTA World players. If you've ever lost a great scene because the chat reset when you reconnected, or spent ages lining up chat text over a screenshot in Photoshop, this is for you.


Both made entirely in WorldKit: a game screenshot plus chat, 550 × 550.

What it does

1. Saves your chat automatically
WorldKit runs in the background while you play and saves every session's chat to a plain text file on your PC. Sessions are sorted by day, with who was in them, how long they lasted and how many lines.

2. Lets you read and search your logs
Search for text, filter by IC, /me, /do, /b or PMs, or show only certain characters. Select lines to copy them or turn them into an image.

3. Makes chat screenshots that look like the game
Pick lines and click Extract image. You get a transparent PNG drawn like the in-game chat: same font (Arial or Mukta), same chat scale, same colors and shadow, wrapped at the same width. Set it to match your own chat settings, and add a dark or light box behind each line if you like.

4. Lets you fix the text right on the image
Typo in the scene? Click into the picture and type. Select words to make them italic or recolor them, drag lines into a different order, or merge two lines one person said. Ctrl+Z undoes anything. Your saved log is never changed.

5. Builds the whole RP shot
The Screenshot Editor puts your game screenshots and chat images together. Pick a canvas (550 × 550, 1080p, or any size), add layers, then move, resize, rotate and fade them. Export a PNG or JPEG ready to post.

Is it safe?

  • Everything stays on your PC. WorldKit doesn't connect to the internet and never uploads your logs.
  • It doesn't touch the game. It reads a copy of FiveM's saved data, never the live files, and doesn't inject anything or run inside FiveM.
  • Your logs, chat images and settings live in %AppData%\WorldKit. Delete that folder and they're gone.

Download

Grab WorldKit-Setup.exe from the latest release:
https://github.com/vuenno/WorldKit/releases/latest

Install it, start it, and play as usual. Your chat saves as you go. It needs Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) and can start with Windows if you want.
